## 🖋️ About the Game

**INKBOUND: THE LAST REEL** is a 2D action-platformer and boss-rush game set inside a mysterious animated film that has come to life.

The world of Inkbound exists inside an ancient cartoon reel known as **The Last Reel**.

For generations, the world was powered by a mysterious substance called **Living Ink** — a supernatural ink capable of giving characters, environments and entire stories life.

Now the film is collapsing.

Frames are disappearing.

Colors are fading.

Characters are losing pieces of their animation.

Entire locations are being erased.

Behind it all stands an enigmatic entity known as:

# 🎥 THE DIRECTOR

The Director believes the world is too corrupted to save.

His solution?

**Erase everything and start again.**

The player takes control of **Milo Inkwhistle**, a small but fearless animated character who must travel through the remaining worlds, recover the legendary **Six Master Frames**, defeat corrupted creatures and confront The Director before the final frame disappears.

# 🎮 Gameplay

INKBOUND focuses heavily on **precision, movement, reaction and pattern recognition**.

The gameplay loop is:

```text
EXPLORE
   ↓
FIGHT
   ↓
LEARN
   ↓
DODGE
   ↓
PARRY
   ↓
DEFEAT
   ↓
REWARD
   ↓
UPGRADE
   ↓
DISCOVER
   ↓
REPEAT
```

The goal is to make every encounter feel challenging but fair.

The player should think:

> "I almost had it."

Then:

> "Now I understand the pattern."

And finally:

> "I DID IT."

# 🧠 Design Philosophy

INKBOUND is built around five major principles:

### 1. Responsive

Every action must react immediately.

### 2. Readable

Players should understand attacks and hazards.

### 3. Challenging

Mastery should be required.

### 4. Fair

Deaths should teach rather than frustrate.

### 5. Memorable

Every world, boss and character should have its own identity.
